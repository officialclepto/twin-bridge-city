TWIN BRIDGE CITY - ON IPHONE / IPAD

OPTION 1 - PLAY IN SAFARI (free, easiest, great for sharing)
  1. Open the web link in Safari:  https://officialclepto.github.io/twin-bridge-city/
  2. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
  3. Open it from the home screen icon. It runs full screen, turn the phone sideways.

OPTION 2 - INSTALL THE IPHONE APP (free Apple ID, needs a computer + USB cable)
  1. On a Windows or Mac computer, download TwinBridgeCity.ipa from
     https://github.com/officialclepto/twin-bridge-city/releases/latest
  2. Install Sideloadly from https://sideloadly.io
     (Windows: also install iTunes and iCloud from Apple's website, NOT the Microsoft Store versions.)
  3. Plug in the iPhone, tap "Trust This Computer" on the phone.
  4. Open Sideloadly, drag TwinBridgeCity.ipa into it, type your Apple ID email, press Start.
     Enter your Apple ID password / 2-factor code if it asks. (Using a spare Apple ID is fine.)
  5. On the iPhone: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > tap your Apple ID > Trust.
     On iOS 16 or newer also turn on Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode (the phone restarts).
  6. Open Twin Bridge City.
  Limits of a free Apple ID: the app stops opening after 7 days. Just repeat step 4 (don't delete the app first,
  so your saves stay). Max 3 sideloaded apps at once.

OPTION 3 - TESTFLIGHT (best for sharing the real app, $99/year Apple Developer account)
  Up to 10,000 people install it with a link through Apple's TestFlight app. Ask Claude to set it up once you have the account.
